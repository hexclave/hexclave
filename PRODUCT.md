# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Developers and product teams use Hexclave to configure and operate the infrastructure around their users, including authentication, teams, payments, email, analytics, and related workflows. Internal Hexclave operators also use concise administration surfaces to review and manage customer-facing output.

## Product Purpose

Hexclave gives product teams one coherent platform for user infrastructure. The dashboard should make consequential configuration and operational work clear, safe, and efficient while keeping the underlying system inspectable.

## Brand Personality

Precise, capable, and direct. Interfaces should feel calm and trustworthy, with concise language and enough technical detail for an expert to understand what will happen.

## Anti-references

Avoid decorative SaaS dashboards, ambiguous controls, novelty interactions, and visual styling that competes with the task. Do not hide important errors or irreversible consequences behind transient feedback.

## Design Principles

- Make system state and the next available action immediately legible.
- Prefer consistent dashboard primitives and familiar interaction patterns.
- Keep expert workflows concise without sacrificing explicit loading, error, and conflict states.
- Treat customer-visible changes as deliberate operations with clear lifecycle boundaries.
- Preserve inspectability: authored content, generated output, and stored state should be reviewable.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use semantic controls, visible keyboard focus, readable contrast, descriptive labels, and reduced-motion-safe behavior. Important errors and blocking decisions must remain visible until resolved.
